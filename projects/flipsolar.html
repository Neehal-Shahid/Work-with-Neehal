Visit Live Site →

The Challenge

What the business needed.

Flip Solar needed a website that could explain solar benefits, show real project proof, and turn curious visitors into contact-form leads — across home, commercial, and industrial buyers.

The site had to build trust fast: after-sale support, monitoring, and clear service paths matter as much as nice visuals in this market.

The Approach

How it was solved.

We built a WordPress site organized around buyer intent: benefits, services by segment, project galleries, testimonials, and a solar load calculator to engage serious prospects.

Contact CTAs repeat through the journey. Office locations and contact details support local trust for Lahore and Dubai presence.

Mobile-first layout and image weight kept the experience usable for people researching solar on their phones.
