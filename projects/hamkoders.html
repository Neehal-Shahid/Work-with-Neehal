The Challenge

What the business needed.

Hamkoders needed a site that matched the quality of their design and development work — something that could close the “show me your portfolio” gap with prospects across Pakistan, Austria, and beyond.

The goal was clear: a modern agency presence with strong service storytelling, featured projects, and a frictionless path to start a conversation.

The Approach

How it was solved.

We built a full WordPress site centered on work and conversion. Hero messaging leads with outcomes, services are structured for scanners, and the portfolio section does the selling with real case studies.

Layout, typography, and motion were tuned so the site feels premium on desktop and stays fast and readable on mobile — where many first visits happen.

Clear CTAs (“Start a Project”, contact) sit throughout the journey without cluttering the design.
